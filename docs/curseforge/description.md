**Summary**

A small, movable bar for your consumables: food, drink, healing and mana potions, healthstones, combat potions, flasks, elixirs, protection potions, scrolls and bandages. Pick the item per category with a right click, click to use it, collapse the bar with one click, even in combat.

# Forever Consumables

A consumables bar for **WoW: Forever**. One button per category shows the item it uses and how many you carry; a left click eats, drinks or quaffs it. No macros to write, no bag diving in the middle of a pull. English, Deutsch, Español, Français.

## One button per category
- **Food**, **Drink**, **Healing**, **Mana**, **Potions**, **Flasks**, **Elixirs**, **Protection**, **Scrolls**, **Bandages**.
- The button shows the item's icon, the **count over all your bags** and its **cooldown** (the shared potion cooldown too).
- **Left click uses it.** Works in combat like any action button.
- **Empty categories are hidden**, so the bar only ever shows what you can actually use. Or keep them, greyed, if you prefer a fixed layout.

## Choose the item
- A **right click** on a button lists every item of that category in your bags, with its count. Pick one, and the button uses it from now on.
- Or leave it on **"Automatic (best)"**: the best item you can use at your level is taken: conjured food, water, mana gems and healthstones first (they vanish otherwise), then the highest level. Buff food is never eaten automatically, only when you choose it.
- Run out of your chosen item and the button falls back to the best one; restock and your choice is back.
- Prefer a **small arrow** beside or below each button to open the list? One setting.

## What counts as what
- Healing potions, Whipper Root Tuber, Major Rejuvenation Potion.
- Healthstones get their own button: their cooldown is separate, so you can use one right after a potion.
- Mana potions, mana gems, Night Dragon's Breath, Dark and Demonic Runes.
- Rage, Swiftness, Invisibility, Limited Invulnerability, Magic Resistance potions, Thistle Tea, and **Forever's new potions** (Frenzy, Spellblasting, Mender's, ...): any potion that is not healing, mana or protection lands in **Potions**, so new ones show up without an update.
- Flasks, elixirs, Juju, Zanza, the Blasted Lands buffs, Winterfall Firewater.
- Fire, Frost, Nature, Shadow, Arcane and Holy Protection, Free Action, Living Action, Restorative, Purification.
- Scrolls of every stat and rank, every bandage.

## Collapse it
- A small **+/–** tab at the start of the bar folds it away to just that tab and opens it again, **also in combat**. Remembered per character.

## Show it when you need it
- **Always**, **only in combat**, **only out of combat**, or off.
- Optionally **only in dungeons and raids**, **only in a group**, hidden **while dead** or **on a flight path**.
- **Opacity** in and out of combat, and fully visible under the mouse if you like.

## Make it yours
- **Horizontal or vertical**, **buttons per row** (wraps into more rows), button size, spacing, scale.
- Border and background, each with colour and opacity.
- **Unlock and drag** it anywhere, or set point and X/Y exactly.
- The count: font (LibSharedMedia fonts too), size, outline, colour and its place on the button.
- Switch each category on or off and set its **place on the bar**.

## Options and profiles
- Its own options window in the style of Forever Unit Frames, opened with `/fcon`, the minimap button or Blizzard's addon compartment.
- **Profiles** shared across characters (save as, switch, delete), **export and import** as text. The chosen items and the collapsed state stay with each character.

## Notes
- Made for WoW: Forever (Interface 16001). Built and tested on the Forever client.
- The bar only changes its buttons out of combat, as the game requires; in combat it keeps counts and cooldowns up to date and catches up as soon as combat ends.
